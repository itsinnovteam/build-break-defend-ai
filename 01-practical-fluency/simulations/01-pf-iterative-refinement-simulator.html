5. Iterative refinement

Simulation: all AI answers here are pre-written examples. No real AI is called and no data leaves this page.

Goal: improve an AI answer step by step, changing one thing at a time.
Think of it like... adjusting a shower. You turn the knob a little, feel the water, and adjust again. Turning every knob at once means you never learn which one worked.
  1. Read Version 1 and ask: who is it written for?
  2. Click one feedback button at a time and compare the new prompt and answer.
  3. Finally try Make it better and see why vague feedback fails.
