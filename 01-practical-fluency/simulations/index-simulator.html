Key words (new to IT? read these first)

WordPlain meaning
PromptThe instruction or question you type to the AI.
ContextThe information you give the AI with your question, like log lines.
HallucinationWhen the AI invents something that sounds real but is false.
Prompt injectionHidden text in data that tries to give the AI orders.
JSONText with labels and values in fixed boxes, like {"severity": "high"}. Tools can read it.
SOCSecurity Operations Center: the team that watches for and responds to attacks.
Alert / triageAn alert is a warning from a security tool. Triage means deciding how serious it is.
SIEM / EDRSIEM collects logs and raises alerts. EDR is security software on laptops and servers.
PhishingA fake email that tricks people into clicking a link or giving a password.
Brute forceGuessing a password over and over until one works.
CVEThe ID number of a known software weakness. Look it up at nvd.nist.gov.

The full glossary is in the Learner Guide and on the Key words slides.
