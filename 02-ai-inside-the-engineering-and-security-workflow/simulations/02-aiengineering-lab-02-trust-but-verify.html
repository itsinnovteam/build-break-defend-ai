Think of it like... A new colleague's work. Day one you check everything; after months of good work on simple tasks you spot-check; for anything that touches production you always double-check. Trust is earned per task type, not given once.
How to play: Move the slider to set how much you would trust AI-written code with no review. Read what that level means.
