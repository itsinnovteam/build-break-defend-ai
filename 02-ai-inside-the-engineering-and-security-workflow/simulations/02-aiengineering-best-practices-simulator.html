Section 1 - Best practices for using AI assistants

Simulation 1 - Safe & Effective Prompting

Practise the two habits that matter most: protect sensitive data, and give the AI a clear, constrained task.

Part A - Redact code and errors before you paste

Module 1 taught you to redact alerts. Code, configs and error messages hide secrets too - often more of them.

Think of it like... Sending a sample to an outside lab. You send the blood sample, not your wallet and house keys. The lab needs the evidence, not your identity.
How to play: A developer on the SOAR team wants AI help with this error from a failing integration script. Click every item that must not leave the company (there are 6). Click again to undo. Keep the details the AI needs to debug it. Then press Check.

Part B - Pick the better prompt

Think of it like... Ordering a made-to-measure barong from a tailor. Give your measurements, the fabric and the event date, or you get a standard size that almost fits.
How to play: For each situation, choose the prompt that follows best practice for engineering work. The feedback names the R-C-T-F-C part (Role, Context, Task, Format, Constraints, from Module 1) or the practice it shows.
