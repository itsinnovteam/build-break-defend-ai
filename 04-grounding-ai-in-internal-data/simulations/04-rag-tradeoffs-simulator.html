Part D - Local or hosted model?

Local (Ollama on your VM, as in Lab 4)Hosted API (cloud provider)
Where data goesStays on the VMLeaves your network; check contract, region, retention
CostNo per-token cost; you pay for the serverPer token (Module 3); grows with top-k and chunk size
Speed on a CPU VMOften 30 to 120 s per answerUsually a few seconds
Answer qualityGood with small models (8B); weaker reasoningUsually stronger models
Fits local-only dataYesNo - hold it back (Section 4.4)
In the SOC: playbooks and incident notes with host names may be local-only - a local model can use them. In DevOps: public runbooks can go to a faster hosted model if the contract allows it.
