AI Bootcamp - Module 4 - Section 4.1

Simulation 1 - Why RAG

The model alone guesses about your company. Grounded in your documents, it answers with facts you can check.

Part A - Closed book or open book?

Think of it like... an exam. The model alone sits a closed-book exam about a company it has never worked at. RAG makes it an open-book exam: we hand it the right pages of OUR handbook first, and it must quote them.
Question from the module brief:
Ask the model alone first. Then click each highlighted claim and decide: is it from our policy, or a guess?

Part B - When the documents do not have the answer

Part C - RAG, paste, fine-tune, system prompt - or the tool itself?

Think of it like... a new hire. RAG = give them the handbook and tell them to look things up. Pasting = hand them one page for one task. System prompt = tell them how to write. Fine-tuning = months of training that changes their habits, not their knowledge of this week's facts. And some questions are best answered by the tool itself.
Tenable and ClamAV are learning examples. Our security toolset has many more tools (EDR, SIEM, firewalls, email security, cloud posture); the same decisions apply to all of them.
