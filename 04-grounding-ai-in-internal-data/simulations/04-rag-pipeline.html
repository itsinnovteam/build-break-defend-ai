Simulation 2 - The RAG pipeline

Follow real Lab 4 data through the indexing pipeline and the query-time pipeline.

Part A - The indexing pipeline (runs once, and again when a document changes)

Think of it like... a library preparing books. The librarian checks each book's label (load), removes torn pages (clean), cuts it into index cards that each keep the book's shelf and access label (chunk), gives every card coordinates on a "meaning map" (embed), and files the cards in a cabinet (store).

Part B - Embeddings: meaning as numbers

An embedding turns text into a list of numbers (all-minilm: 384 numbers) so that texts with similar meaning get similar numbers. Cosine similarity measures how close two vectors point: 1.00 = same direction, about 0 = unrelated. These are the real all-minilm values from Lab 4 (embed_demo.py).

A 2-D map of the 384-D vectors (for teaching only)

Click any two points. Compare how close they look on the map with their real similarity.
Warning. Squeezing 384 dimensions into 2 (PCA here, t-SNE or UMAP elsewhere) always distorts distances. A pretty cluster plot is not proof that retrieval works. Measure with real questions (Section 4.3).

Part C - The query-time pipeline (runs for every question)

1 embed the question → 2 filter by the user's roles (metadata filtering) → 3 rank by similarity → 4 keep the top-k above 0.30 → 5 build the context → 6 the model answers with citations.
