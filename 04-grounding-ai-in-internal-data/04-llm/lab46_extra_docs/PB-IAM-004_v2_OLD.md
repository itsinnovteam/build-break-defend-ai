# Suspicious Login and Account Compromise Playbook

FICTIONAL TRAINING DOCUMENT. Northwind SOC. OLD VERSION, replaced by v3.2.

## Escalation path

- All successful logins from an unusual country: escalate to Tier 2 within
  2 hours by email to the SOC shared mailbox.
- Privileged accounts: notify the IR Lead within 60 minutes. A major
  incident bridge is not required.

## Containment

- Tier 1 must not reset passwords. Ask the user to change their own password.
