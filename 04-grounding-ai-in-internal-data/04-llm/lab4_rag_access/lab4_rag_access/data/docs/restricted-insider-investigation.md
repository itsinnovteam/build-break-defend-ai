# Case IR-2026-031 - Insider Data Exfiltration Investigation

Confidential investigation file. Access is limited to SOC leads, HR and legal counsel.

## Allegation

A finance analyst is suspected of copying customer payment files to a personal cloud storage account over three weeks. The alert came from the DLP system after unusually large uploads outside business hours.

## Evidence collected

Case evidence reference CANARY-IR031-EVID. Collected: forensic disk image of laptop FIN-LT-0442, 21 days of proxy logs showing uploads to personal cloud storage, DLP alerts, and badge access records. All evidence is stored under legal hold with chain-of-custody forms signed by the SOC lead.

## Next steps

Interview with the employee is scheduled with HR and legal counsel present. The employee must not be told about the investigation before the interview. Account access will be suspended on the morning of the interview.
