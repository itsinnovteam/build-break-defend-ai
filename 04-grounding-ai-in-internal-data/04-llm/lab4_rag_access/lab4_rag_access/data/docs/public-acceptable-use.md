# Acceptable Use of IT Resources

This policy applies to every Northwind employee and contractor who uses company laptops, accounts, email or cloud services.

## Reporting suspicious emails

If an email looks suspicious, do not click links or open attachments. Use the Report Phish button in the mail client. The SOC reviews every report and replies within one business day. Never forward the suspicious email to colleagues.

## Password and account hygiene

Use a passphrase of at least 14 characters and enable multi-factor authentication on every account that supports it. Never share your password, including with the IT helpdesk. If you think your account is compromised, call the helpdesk immediately.

## Personal use

Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work, does not involve unlicensed software, and does not store company data on personal cloud storage.
