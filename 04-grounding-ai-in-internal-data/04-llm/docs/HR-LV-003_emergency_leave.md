# Emergency Leave Policy

FICTIONAL TRAINING DOCUMENT. Northwind HR. Not a real policy.

## Eligibility

All regular employees may take up to 3 days of emergency leave per calendar
year for a sudden family emergency, illness of a dependent, or disaster
affecting the employee's home.

## How to request

1. Notify your direct manager by phone or chat before the start of your shift,
   or as soon as you safely can.
2. File form HR-F12 "Emergency Leave Request" in the HR portal within
   2 working days after you return.
3. Attach supporting documents (for example a medical certificate or a
   barangay certificate) if the leave is longer than 1 day.

## Approval path

Your direct manager approves the request in the HR portal. Leave longer than
3 days needs approval from the department head.
