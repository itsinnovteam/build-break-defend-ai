# Emergency Change Procedure

FICTIONAL TRAINING DOCUMENT. Northwind IT. Not a real procedure.

## When to use

Use an emergency change only when waiting for the weekly Change Advisory
Board (CAB) would cause a security incident, an outage, or a breach of a
regulatory deadline. A security patch with a 72-hour SLA qualifies.

## Approval

- An emergency change needs approval from the on-call Duty Manager and one
  member of the Emergency CAB (ECAB).
- Approval may be given by phone, but the approver must confirm in the
  change ticket within 24 hours.

## Steps

1. Raise a change ticket of type "Emergency" with the business reason, the
   rollback plan, and the affected services.
2. Get ECAB and Duty Manager approval.
3. Implement the change and record the start and end time.
4. Present the change at the next regular CAB meeting for review.
