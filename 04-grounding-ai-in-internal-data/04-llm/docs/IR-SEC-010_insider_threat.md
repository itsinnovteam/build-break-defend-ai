# Insider Threat Investigation Procedure

FICTIONAL TRAINING DOCUMENT. Northwind SOC. Not a real procedure.
RESTRICTED: Incident Response Leads only.

## Scope

Use this procedure when an investigation involves a current employee or
contractor who is suspected of deliberately misusing access.

## Rules of the investigation

- Only IR Leads may run or view this investigation. Tier 1 and Tier 2
  analysts must hand over to the IR Lead and stop.
- Do not alert the subject. Do not disable the account without Legal and HR
  approval.
- Store all evidence in the restricted IR evidence share with chain-of-custody
  forms IR-F03.

## Escalation

Notify Legal and the HR Business Partner within 24 hours of opening the case.
