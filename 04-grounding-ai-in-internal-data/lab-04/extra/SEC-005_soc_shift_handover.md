# SOC shift handover checklist

The SOC works in three shifts: 06:00 to 14:00, 14:00 to 22:00 and 22:00 to 06:00.
At every handover the outgoing analyst updates all open P1 and P2 cases in the case queue,
lists any hosts still isolated in EDR, and names the analyst who owns each open case.

The incoming analyst confirms the handover in the SOC channel within 15 minutes.
