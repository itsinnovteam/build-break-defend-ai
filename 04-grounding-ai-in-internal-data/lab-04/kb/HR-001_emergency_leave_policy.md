# Emergency leave policy

Emergency leave is for sudden, serious events you could not plan for, such as the
hospitalisation or death of a close family member, or a fire or flood at your home.
Every regular employee has up to 3 working days of paid emergency leave per calendar year.
Unused emergency leave does not carry over to the next year.

How to apply: tell your direct manager as soon as possible, and at the latest 2 hours after
your shift starts, by phone or Teams chat. Then file form HR-F-12 (Emergency Leave Request)
in the PeopleDesk portal within 3 working days after you return to work.

For emergency leave of more than 1 day, attach a supporting document to form HR-F-12, for
example a hospital certificate or a death certificate. Your manager approves the request in
PeopleDesk, and HR checks it. Questions: HR helpdesk, extension 2100, hr-help@example.com.

HR processing checklist (HR staff only): check the employee's remaining balance in
PeopleDesk, verify the supporting document within 5 working days, and store medical
documents only in the HR case system, never in email. Requests longer than 3 days go to
the HR manager as special leave.
