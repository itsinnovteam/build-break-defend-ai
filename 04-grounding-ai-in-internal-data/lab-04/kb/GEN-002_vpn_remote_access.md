# VPN and remote access

To work from home, connect with the company VPN client before you open any internal
system. Sign in with your company account and approve the push notification in the
authenticator app (multi-factor authentication).

Contractors get VPN access for the length of their contract only. Their access is
reviewed every 30 days by the sponsoring manager.

If the VPN client shows error 809 or cannot connect, restart your home router first,
then contact the IT Service Desk at extension 4357 with a screenshot of the error.
