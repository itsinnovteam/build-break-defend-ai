# Password reset and account lockout

Forgot your password? Use the self-service portal at https://passwords.example.com.
You need your registered mobile number for the one-time code. The new password must be
at least 14 characters and must not reuse any of your last 10 passwords.

Your account locks after 5 wrong password attempts in 15 minutes. It unlocks by itself
after 30 minutes. If you cannot wait, call the IT Service Desk at extension 4357.

The Service Desk will never ask for your password, by phone, chat or email.
