# How to report a security incident (everyone)

If you think your laptop is infected, or you see ransomware or a ransom note, unplug the
network cable or turn off Wi-Fi, do not shut the laptop down, and call the IT Service Desk
at extension 4357 straight away. The Service Desk is staffed 24 hours a day and passes the
call to the SOC.

Report suspicious emails with the Report Phish button in Outlook. Do not forward them.
