# Handling medical and family documents (HR only)

Medical certificates and death certificates are sensitive personal information. Only HR
staff may open them. Store them in the HR case system under the employee's case number,
for example case ER-2026-0147 (fake), and nowhere else.

Keep these documents for 3 years after the leave ends, then delete them. Never paste their
contents into a cloud AI tool, a chat or an email.
