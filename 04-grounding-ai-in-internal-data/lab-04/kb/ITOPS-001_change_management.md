# Change management procedure

Every change to a production system needs a change request (CR) in the ITSM tool.
Standard changes are pre-approved, low-risk tasks from the standard change catalog.
Normal changes are reviewed by the Change Advisory Board (CAB), which meets every
Tuesday at 14:00. Submit a normal change by Monday 17:00 to be on that week's agenda.

Emergency changes fix a live outage or an actively exploited vulnerability. They are
approved by the on-call IT Operations manager by phone, and the CR is completed within
24 hours after the fix.

Change freeze: no normal changes from 15 December to 5 January, and during month-end
closing (last 2 working days of each month). Every CR needs a tested rollback plan.
