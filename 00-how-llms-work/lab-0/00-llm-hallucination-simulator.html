What is a hallucination?

A hallucination is when a model states something false or unverifiable as if it were a confirmed fact with no change in tone to warn you. Fluent delivery is not proof of accuracy.

An easy way to picture it: think of a friend who never says "I don't know." Ask them anything, and they'll give you a confident, detailed answer whether they actually know it or just made it up on the spot. Their tone doesn't change either way. That's the real risk with hallucination: you can't tell which answer you got just by how sure it sounds.

There's no score here and no wrong way to go through it, try to spot the fabricated answers before reading the tags, but reading the tags first is fine too. The goal is just to notice the pattern once, so it's easier to catch in real use.

How to use this

  1. Pick a scenario from the dropdown, each represents a different situation that commonly triggers hallucination.
  2. Read the fixed prompt; this is exactly what was asked.
  3. Click "Show Model Responses" and try to guess which answers are fabricated before reading the tags.
  4. Compare against Ground Truth to see what's actually verified.
  5. Check History anytime to review which scenarios you've covered.
