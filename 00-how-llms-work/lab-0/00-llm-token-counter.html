Estimate starts from the GPT-2/GPT-4 pretokenization pattern (splits on words, numbers, punctuation, whitespace), then applies a morphological heuristic — stripping common prefixes/suffixes (-ization, -tion, -able, un-, re-, etc.) — to guess where long or complex words would further split under real byte-pair encoding. This is an approximation: real tokenizers use a trained merge list of ~50,000–100,000 entries, so some words shown here as one token may actually split, and vice versa. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. For an exact count against a specific model, use that provider's official tokenizer.
