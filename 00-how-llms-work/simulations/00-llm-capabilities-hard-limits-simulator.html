What is a "hard limit"?

Some things an LLM struggles with aren't occasional mistakes a better prompt can fix , they're structural limits baked into how the technology works. Knowing which is which tells you when to trust the output, and when you must verify it yourself.

An easy way to picture it: imagine a brilliant, widely-read writer taking a closed-book exam. They can explain complex ideas clearly, summarize a stack of documents in seconds, and reason through a problem step by step , but they can't leave the room to check today's news, there's no calculator on the desk, and by tomorrow's exam they won't remember a word of this one. That's an LLM: exceptional with language and reasoning, closed-book on live facts, exact math, and memory across sessions.
