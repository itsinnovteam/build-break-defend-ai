The OWASP LLM Top 10

OWASP publishes the ten most important risks for LLM applications. It is the common vocabulary between engineers and security reviewers: instead of arguing from scratch, you both point at the same list and ask "are we covered for each one?"

Analogy - a pre-flight checklist. Pilots do not reinvent safety each flight; they run the same list every time. OWASP LLM Top 10 is the pre-flight checklist for shipping an AI feature.
In this system: the headline risk is LLM01 Prompt injection (the poisoned ticket from Lab 5) and LLM06 Excessive agency (an agent with too many tools). Both are already addressed by the Lab 5 controls.

Coverage - and honest gaps

A good review shows what is not applicable too. Here, LLM04 (model poisoning), LLM07 (system-prompt leakage) and LLM08 (vector weaknesses) are marked N/A by design - we do not train the model, keep no secrets in the prompt, and use no vector store. Documented non-applicability is a strength, not a gap.
