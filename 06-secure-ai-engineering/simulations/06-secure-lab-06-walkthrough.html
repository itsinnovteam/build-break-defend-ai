Lab 6 - make the Lab 5 agent deployment-grade

Lab 6 adds no new agent code. It produces the Threat Model & Control Document that lets you defend the Lab 5 agent in front of a CISO - all generated from one machine-readable model, with every control backed by a real Lab 5 test. Every output below is from the real lab on Python 3.9 - no GPU and no model needed. All data is fictional.

The idea that ties it together: the threat model, the control matrix and the compliance mapping are all generated from system_model.py. The paperwork cannot drift from the build, and nothing is claimed that is not proven by a test.
