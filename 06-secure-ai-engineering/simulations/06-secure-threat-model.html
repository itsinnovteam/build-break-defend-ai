Threat-modelling an AI system

A threat model answers four questions a CISO will ask, in order: what is the system, where does untrusted input get in, what could go wrong, and what stops it? You do not need a heavy tool - you need the data flow drawn honestly and a decision for every threat.

Analogy - a building security walk-through. A guard does not guess. They walk the floor plan, mark every door and window (the ways in), ask "what could someone do here?", and check each one has a lock or a reason it is safe. A threat model is that walk-through for your data flow.
SOC / DevOps: the system here is the Lab 5 triage agent. Untrusted input enters in two places - what the model proposes, and the ticket comments it reads. Those two doors are where the whole review focuses.
